How grades calculate

Only percent scores and credit hours feed the GPA engine; editing either column recomputes Grade Pt, Grade, and Quality Pts instantly using the ladder (quality points = grade point × credits earned).

Course identifiers

Subject / Course Number / Description labels are descriptive only and never enter the GPA math.

Term aggregates

  • Attempted / Earned / GPA Hours: sum each course's credited hours across the semester (numerically identical in this workbook).
  • Quality Points: sum each row's Quality Pts.
  • Term GPA: Quality Points ÷ GPA Hours for that semester.

Cumulative totals

  • Roll the aggregates above forward across each listed semester.
  • Cumulative GPA: cumulative Quality Points ÷ cumulative GPA Hours.
